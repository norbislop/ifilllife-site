Some days are full before they even begin. Messages, errands, unfinished ideas, and other people’s plans all compete for a place in them.

Before adding another thing to the list, take a moment to look at what is already there.

## Name what deserves your attention

Write down the things on your mind. Then mark one that matters to you, one that has a real deadline, and one that could wait.

These may overlap. The point is to see the difference between what feels urgent and what you actually want to make room for.

## Leave a little breathing room

A useful plan can include open space. You might leave an evening unscheduled, make a task smaller, or decide when you will stop working on it.

The question is not how much you can squeeze in. It is what you want this day to hold.

## Notice what changed

At the end of the day, write one sentence about what helped and one about what you would adjust.

Keep the reflection simple. You are collecting a little more understanding, not grading yourself.
